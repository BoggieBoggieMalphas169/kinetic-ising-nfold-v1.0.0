# References

Core references motivating the algorithms used in this project:

1. N. Metropolis, A. W. Rosenbluth, M. N. Rosenbluth, A. H. Teller, and E. Teller, **“Equation of State Calculations by Fast Computing Machines,”** *The Journal of Chemical Physics* **21**, 1087–1092 (1953). DOI: `10.1063/1.1699114`.
2. R. J. Glauber, **“Time-Dependent Statistics of the Ising Model,”** *Journal of Mathematical Physics* **4**, 294–307 (1963).
3. A. B. Bortz, M. H. Kalos, and J. L. Lebowitz, **“A new algorithm for Monte Carlo simulation of Ising spin systems,”** *Journal of Computational Physics* **17**(1), 10–18 (1975). DOI: `10.1016/0021-9991(75)90060-1`.

The project materials also include teaching/review notes used during development to define the ten-class n-fold construction, the metastable first-passage protocol, and the distinction between Monte Carlo time and computational time.
